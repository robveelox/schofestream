Schofestream 0.1.5 — Continue Watching fix

What changed
------------
Continue Watching no longer uses Jellyfin's /Items/Resume endpoint. Jellyfin can
hide very recently started content according to its resume thresholds.

Schofestream now requests the user's recently played unfinished Movies/Episodes,
then keeps every item whose saved PlaybackPositionTicks is greater than zero.
The dashboard shows up to 20 of them, newest first.

Upgrade
-------
Overwrite your existing Schofestream files with this package and hard refresh
the browser (Cmd+Shift+R on macOS). No Caddy changes are required.

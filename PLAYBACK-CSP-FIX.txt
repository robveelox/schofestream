Schofestream 0.1.3 playback CSP fix
=================================

The console errors:
  Refused to create a worker from blob:...
  Refused to load media from data:,WEBVTT

were caused by Schofestream's own Content-Security-Policy header.

0.1.3 changes it to allow:
  worker-src 'self' blob:
  media-src 'self' https://player.schofestream.co.uk blob: data:

No Caddy change is required for these particular errors.

After uploading 0.1.3, hard-refresh the browser (Cmd+Shift+R on macOS).
The JS URLs should show ?v=0.1.3 in DevTools.
